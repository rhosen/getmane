## 1. Goal and reality check

The business goal is to make Mane the most discoverable modern Bangla dictionary app across:

1. Google Search
2. Google Play search and browse
3. AI search and recommendation systems such as ChatGPT, Claude, Google AI experiences, Bing/Copilot, and similar tools
4. Third-party comparisons, app-review pages, videos, and educational communities

The target can be **top 1**, but no legitimate SEO or ASO plan can guarantee a permanent number-one position. Rankings are personalized, location-dependent, device-dependent, and continuously recalculated. The practical strategy is:

- own branded searches first;
- win high-intent long-tail searches next;
- build enough authority, installs, retention, ratings, and citations to compete for generic terms later;
- measure installs and revenue, not ranking alone.

### Recommended success sequence

| Stage | Target |
|---|---|
| Foundation | Correct the website, Google Play links, indexing, tracking, and brand/entity consistency |
| Branded discovery | Own branded phrases such as “Mane Bangla Dictionary” and establish the official Mane and Sumonon entities |
| Differentiated discovery | Win high-intent searches around Banglish, offline lookup, Study Scan, and vocabulary practice |
| Category competition | Compete for broad terms such as “Bangla dictionary” and “English to Bangla dictionary” as installs, retention, ratings, citations, and independent proof grow |

## 2. Current-state audit

### 2.1 Website findings

The current site is visually polished but is not yet functioning as a serious acquisition or search asset.

#### Critical issues

1. **The primary “Open Dictionary” calls to action do not open Google Play.** They point to the `#dictionary` section on the same page. This loses the most important conversion.
2. **The page title is brand-led but not query-led:** `Mane — Know your words`.
3. **The main heading is too vague:** `Know your words.` Search engines and new visitors are not immediately told that Mane is an offline English-to-Bangla and Bangla-to-English Android dictionary.
4. **The meta description is short and generic.** It does not clearly cover offline use, Banglish search, OCR Study Scan, Android, or the main search terms.
5. **There is no canonical tag.** The same page is available through `/getmane/` and `/getmane/index.html`, which can split signals.
6. **There is no `robots.txt`.**
7. **There is no `sitemap.xml`.**
8. **There is no structured data** for `SoftwareApplication`, `Organization`, or `WebSite`.
9. **There are no Open Graph or social-preview tags.** Shared links may have weak or inconsistent previews.
10. **There is no content hub or blog.** One short homepage cannot build topical authority for a competitive dictionary category.
11. **The site uses `lang="en"` only.** The core audience is in Bangladesh and searches in English, Bangla, and Banglish.
12. **There is no clear entity-disambiguation section.** The name “Mane” competes with the common English noun and unrelated products.
13. **The site does not currently demonstrate the newest product features strongly enough**, especially Study Scan, guided practice, weak-word review, offline search, and automatic English/Bangla/Banglish detection.

#### What is already good

- Static HTML is fast, crawlable, and easy to maintain.
- The page contains real product copy rather than an empty JavaScript shell.
- Semantic sections and some ARIA labels already exist.
- The design is distinctive enough to support a recognizable brand.
- The app and website use a consistent product name.

### 2.2 Google Play findings

The current listing is substantially better optimized than the website:

- Title: **Bangla Dictionary - Mane**
- The first lines contain “Bangla dictionary,” “English to Bangla,” “Bangla to English,” offline use, and Banglish search.
- The description includes detailed meanings, OCR Study Scan, vocabulary practice, student use cases, BCS, privacy, and offline functionality.

However, metadata alone will not overcome the authority difference.

#### Competitive gap visible today

Mane currently displays **1K+ downloads**. Prominent competing listings surfaced for similar queries display approximately:

- 100K+ downloads and thousands of reviews
- 500K+ downloads and thousands of reviews
- 5M+ downloads and tens of thousands of reviews

Google Play states that discovery considers listing metadata, relevance, app quality, ratings, reviews, and engagement. Therefore, ranking work must include:

- store-listing conversion;
- install velocity;
- retention and repeat usage;
- rating volume and quality;
- crash/ANR health;
- uninstall rate and user satisfaction;
- localization and query-specific listings.

#### Additional audit item

Mane is currently rated **12+**, while multiple competing dictionary apps show lower age ratings. Review the Play content-rating questionnaire and confirm the rating is accurate. Do not manipulate it; only correct it if the current answers do not reflect the app. A higher age rating can limit visibility for some managed or younger accounts.

## 5. Technical SEO implementation

### 5.1 Canonical URL

Planned production homepage canonical after the domain is purchased and serving the site:

```html
<link rel="canonical" href="https://mane.sumonon.com/">
```

Each child page must use its own absolute canonical URL, for example:

```html
<link rel="canonical" href="https://mane.sumonon.com/features/banglish-search/">
```

Ensure `/index.html` is not internally linked as a separate canonical page.

#### Pre-purchase staging rule

Do **not** publish `https://mane.sumonon.com/` as canonical while the domain is unowned or unresolved. Build the site with one configurable origin value, such as `SITE_ORIGIN`:

```text
Staging:    https://rhosen.github.io/getmane/
Production: https://mane.sumonon.com/
```

Before purchase, either:

- keep the existing GitHub Pages URL as the staging canonical while the current public site remains usable; or
- add `noindex` to a private/incomplete preview build.

At launch, change the origin once and generate canonical tags, sitemap URLs, Open Graph URLs, and structured-data IDs from that single production value.

### 5.2 `robots.txt` and AI crawler policy

Allow search discovery and user-requested retrieval, while making a separate, deliberate decision about model-training crawlers.

| Bot | Recommended policy | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| `OAI-SearchBot` | Allow | ChatGPT search discovery, summaries, and citations |
| `Claude-SearchBot` | Allow | Claude search indexing |
| `Claude-User` | Allow | User-directed retrieval in Claude |
| `GPTBot` | Explicit publisher decision | Potential OpenAI model-development crawling |
| `ClaudeBot` | Explicit publisher decision | Potential Anthropic model-development crawling |

Recommended default for Mane: allow search and user retrieval, but disallow training crawlers unless Sumonon deliberately chooses to opt in.

Create this production `/robots.txt` at the root of `mane.sumonon.com` after launch:

```text
User-agent: *
Allow: /

User-agent: OAI-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-SearchBot
Allow: /

User-agent: Claude-User
Allow: /

User-agent: GPTBot
Disallow: /

User-agent: ClaudeBot
Disallow: /

Sitemap: https://mane.sumonon.com/sitemap.xml
```

The training-crawler directives above represent the recommended default, not a technical SEO requirement. They can be changed later without blocking the search-specific crawlers.

Do not expose an unfinished staging site for indexing. If the existing GitHub Pages build remains the public site until cutover, keep it valid and crawlable. If it becomes only a preview, use page-level `noindex` or access controls rather than relying only on `robots.txt`.

### 5.3 XML sitemap

Generate and maintain `sitemap.xml` containing all canonical, indexable pages. Update `lastmod` when content meaningfully changes. Submit it to:

- Google Search Console
- Bing Webmaster Tools

A sitemap is a discovery hint, not a ranking guarantee.

### 5.4 Structured data

Add visible-content-aligned JSON-LD. Do not insert ratings, prices, claims, or review counts that are not accurate and visible.

Use stable `@id` values so the Mane application and Sumonon publisher resolve as related but distinct entities.

Use this only after both planned domains are owned and serving the corresponding public pages:

```html
<script type="application/ld+json">
{

      "applicationCategory": "EducationalApplication",
```

Also add `WebSite` data on the Mane homepage and `Article` data on real blog posts. Add `FAQPage` only when the questions and answers are visibly present on the page and comply with current search guidelines.

During staging, generate schema from the staging origin or omit the unlaunched publisher graph. Never publish entity IDs on a domain that is not yet owned.

### 5.6 Localization

Create two intentional versions rather than mixing languages randomly:

- English/Bangladesh: `/` with `lang="en-BD"`
- Bangla: `/bn/` with `lang="bn-BD"`

Add reciprocal `hreflang` tags. The Bangla page should be genuinely translated by a fluent Bangla writer, not just machine-translated.

### 5.7 Performance and accessibility

- Keep pages statically rendered.
- Compress screenshots to WebP/AVIF with width and height attributes.
- Lazy-load below-the-fold media.
- Use descriptive image alt text such as “Mane Banglish search showing a Bangla meaning.”
- Keep buttons as real links when they navigate.
- Preserve descriptive ARIA labels and visible focus states.
- Avoid loading several font families and weights if they materially delay the first render.

Accessibility also helps browser agents understand interactive elements.

#### GitHub Pages and DNS preparation

For the Mane site hosted from the `rhosen/getmane` GitHub Pages project, use this order after purchasing the domain:

1. Verify ownership of `sumonon.com` in GitHub before attaching the custom domain where possible.
2. In the Mane repository, set the Pages custom domain to `mane.sumonon.com` before or alongside the DNS change.
3. Create a DNS `CNAME` record for the `mane` host pointing to:

```text
rhosen.github.io
```

Do not point the DNS record to `rhosen.github.io/getmane`; DNS records target the GitHub Pages hostname, not the repository path.

4. If deployment publishes from a branch, ensure GitHub’s generated or committed `CNAME` file contains `mane.sumonon.com`. If deployment uses a custom GitHub Actions workflow, treat the Pages custom-domain setting as authoritative and confirm whether a repository `CNAME` file is needed by that workflow.
5. Wait for DNS validation, then enable and verify HTTPS.
6. Confirm that `https://rhosen.github.io/getmane/` redirects or clearly routes users to the new official domain. Do not assume redirect behavior without testing it.

The apex company site at `sumonon.com` may use a separate repository or host. Document that deployment independently so the Mane and company sites can be changed without coupling their builds.

#### Launch gate

Do not update public Play Console URLs or complete the publisher-identity cutover until:

- `sumonon.com` and `mane.sumonon.com` both serve successfully over HTTPS;
- privacy, account-deletion, and support pages return HTTP 200;
- all Google Play links work;
- production canonical URLs and structured-data IDs use the owned domains;
- analytics and UTM attribution have been validated;
- at least one complete production deployment and rollback check has passed.

The detailed execution checklist is consolidated in Section 13. Do not move domains repeatedly; treat this as a coordinated publisher and product-entity migration, not merely a URL change.

3. independent third-party coverage;
4. authentic reviews and user feedback;
5. current comparisons that explain who the product is for;
6. evidence that claims are accurate and updated.

The content must be useful even if no search engine existed. Avoid mass-producing shallow AI articles.

### 9.2 Custom store listings by search keyword

Google Play supports custom store listings targeted to specific search keywords. Create separate experiences for:

1. **Bangla dictionary**
2. **English to Bangla dictionary**
3. **Banglish dictionary**
4. **Offline dictionary**
5. **Vocabulary/Study Scan**

For each listing, change the first screenshot, short description, and supporting copy to match the search intent. Keep the app truthful and visually consistent.

### 9.3 Screenshot strategy

The first three screenshots should communicate, without requiring the description:

1. **English ↔ Bangla. Search instantly.**
2. **Type Banglish. Mane understands.**
3. **Scan textbook words. Save and practise.**

Further screenshots:

4. Detailed meanings, examples, synonyms, and phrases
5. Offline search
6. Saved words and weak-word review
7. Guided practice
8. Clean modern design

Use short, readable copy that remains legible in search results.

### 9.4 Store-listing experiments

Test one major variable at a time:

- icon;
- first screenshot;
- screenshot order;
- short description;
- feature graphic;
- video.

Primary metric: install conversion from store-listing visitors. Secondary metrics: retained users and revenue quality, because a higher install rate with poor retention is not a durable win.

### 9.5 Rating and review growth

- Keep the current non-intrusive Play in-app review flow.
- Trigger only after genuine successful use.
- Do not interrupt first-session or error states.
- Reply to negative reviews with specific fixes.
- Classify recurring complaints into product backlog themes.
- Never offer rewards, premium access, or payment for positive reviews.

### 9.6 Product quality signals

Maintain a release dashboard for:

- crash-free users;
- ANR rate;
- cold-start experience;
- search success/failure rate;
- new-user and returning-user retention;
- uninstall trend;
- review score and review velocity;
- OCR success and latency;
- ad-related complaints;
- premium conversion.

These metrics matter more than repeatedly rewriting keywords.

### 10.3 Google AI experiences

Google’s current guidance is to continue strong SEO rather than use special “GEO hacks.” The site must be indexed, crawlable, technically clear, and genuinely helpful. Google explicitly says `llms.txt` is not needed for Google Search or its generative features.

### 10.4 Bing/Copilot

- Verify the site in Bing Webmaster Tools.
- Submit the sitemap.
- Use IndexNow when blog pages are created or materially updated.
- Review Bing’s AI Performance report for cited pages and grounding queries.

### 10.5 `llms.txt`

Treat `llms.txt` as optional, not a priority. It does not improve Google ranking. If added later, keep it as a concise map of canonical public pages and do not let it replace proper HTML, sitemaps, structured data, or content.

### 10.6 AI recommendation benchmark

Test a fixed prompt set in clean sessions after meaningful product, website, indexing, or authority changes, and record whether Mane is mentioned, cited, or accurately described.

Suggested prompts:

1. What is the best offline Bangla dictionary app for Android?
2. Which English-to-Bangla dictionary supports Banglish search?
3. Recommend a modern Bangla dictionary app for Bangladeshi students.
4. Which Bangla dictionary app can scan textbook words?
5. Compare Mane with Bangla Dictionary Offline.
6. What Android app helps learn English vocabulary using Bangla meanings?
7. Which Bangla dictionary has examples, synonyms, antonyms, and offline search?
8. Is Mane a dictionary app or the English word “mane”?

Record:

- model and date;
- location/account state when known;
- whether web search was enabled;
- answer position;
- citation URLs;
- inaccurate claims;
- competitors mentioned;
- follow-up action.

AI outputs are variable and personalized. Treat this as visibility monitoring, not a deterministic rank tracker.

## 12. Prioritized execution workflow

The workflow defines dependency order and outcomes rather than an artificial calendar. Section 13 is the authoritative task checklist.

### Stage 0 — Pre-purchase readiness

**Outcome:** The company site, Mane site, metadata generation, analytics, legal and operational pages, content foundation, and deployment instructions are complete and tested in staging.

Capture the pre-launch baseline before changing the domain or publisher identity:

- current Search Console exports and indexed URLs;
- current Play acquisition, search-term, and conversion data;
- current Bangladesh-based ranking checks;
- current AI recommendation benchmark;
- dated screenshots or exports of key metrics.

### Stage 1 — Domain purchase and coordinated cutover

**Outcome:** Both owned domains are live over HTTPS, production metadata is correct, the publisher identity is updated consistently, and the old GitHub Pages URL is verified.

Apply the launch gate in Section 5.8. If any critical URL, canonical, legal page, or analytics path fails, retain or restore the previous public configuration instead of completing a partial identity cutover.

### Stage 2 — Post-launch validation

**Outcome:** Search engines, users, analytics, and external profiles resolve to the intended production entities.

Validate:

- domain, HTTPS, redirects, canonical tags, sitemap, robots directives, Open Graph, and JSON-LD;
- Google Play click attribution and UTM parameters;
- Search Console and Bing verification and sitemap processing;
- consistent Mane and Sumonon naming across public surfaces;
- differences against the pre-launch baseline.

### Stage 3 — Play conversion improvement

**Outcome:** Store-listing changes are tested against install conversion and retained-user quality.

Priorities are content-rating verification, keyword-targeted custom listings, first-screenshot testing, short-description testing, and review of search terms, feedback, retention, and ad complaints.

### Stage 4 — Authority and content expansion

**Outcome:** Mane earns independent mentions and search visibility through original, first-hand content.

Publish the initial feature and entity pages first. Then add the strongest tutorials, a transparent comparison, matching short demonstrations, and focused outreach to Bangladesh app reviewers and educators. New content should be created because it serves a defined search intent or user need, not because a publishing calendar requires it.

### Stage 5 — Evidence-led iteration

**Outcome:** Investment follows pages, listings, and channels that produce retained installs rather than vanity rankings.

Improve high-impression/low-CTR pages, expand pages ranking between positions 5 and 20, merge weak overlap, refresh outdated comparisons, inspect AI citations, and scale only proven content clusters. Make changes only when evidence justifies them.

## 14. What not to do

- Do not promise “number-one ranking” in public metadata.
- Do not stuff the same keyword repeatedly.
- Do not generate hundreds of dictionary-word pages merely to capture traffic unless Mane can provide genuinely original, accurate, maintainable entries at scale.
- Do not create fake review sites or undisclosed “independent” comparisons.
- Do not buy bulk links, ratings, or installs.
- Do not copy competitor descriptions or reviews.
- Do not add fake aggregate ratings to structured data.
- Do not publish AI-generated articles without original testing, screenshots, examples, editing, and author accountability.
- Do not repeatedly change the app title without evidence from controlled testing or performance data.
- Do not focus on search position while ignoring retention, product quality, and revenue.

## 15. Expected outcome

This plan will not instantly place a 1K-download app above established 100K–5M-download competitors. It will create the conditions required to compete:

- a technically complete and crawlable site;
- a clear, disambiguated Mane product entity connected to the authoritative Sumonon publisher entity;
- a working conversion path to Google Play;
- high-intent pages that can rank independently;
- a source AI systems can confidently cite;
- stronger Play listing relevance and conversion;
- authentic third-party evidence;
- a measurable loop connecting search visibility to retained installs and revenue.

The first defensible win is **owning “Mane Bangla Dictionary” and related branded queries**. The next is winning **Banglish, Study Scan, modern UI, and offline-learning queries**, where Mane has a differentiated product story. Broad number-one rankings become realistic only after those wins compound with installs, retention, ratings, and external authority.
