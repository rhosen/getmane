Mane (মানে) is an Android app for English-to-Bangla and Bangla-to-English dictionary lookup, vocabulary review, and study support. At its core, it is built for people who want a cleaner and more useful dictionary experience than a simple translation box can offer.

Many language apps are good at giving a quick answer once. They are less thoughtful when the same person needs to search again, save a word, come back later, and actually remember what they learned. Mane is designed around that longer relationship with words.

That is why the app combines offline-friendly dictionary use, Banglish input, saved-word review, Study Scan, and vocabulary practice into one focused workflow. The goal is not only to tell someone what a word means. The goal is to help them find it quickly and revisit it when it matters.

## Who Mane is for

Mane is especially suited to Bangla speakers learning English, students working through textbooks and notes, and users who want a dictionary app instead of a general-purpose translator. It also fits learners who want to build a habit of saving and revisiting vocabulary instead of treating every lookup as disposable.

That audience matters because it shapes the product. A student under time pressure needs speed. A repeat learner needs structure. Someone searching from study material needs better capture and review tools than a normal dictionary usually provides.

## What makes it different

What makes Mane different is not the label “dictionary” by itself. It is the combination of fast English and Bangla lookup, Banglish search, study-oriented flows, and review tools that continue after the first search is done.

In practice, that means the app is trying to support both discovery and retention. You can search, understand, save, and return. The product story is stronger because those pieces work together instead of sitting in separate apps or separate modes.

For users, the difference feels simple: fewer interruptions, less switching, and a better path from finding a word to remembering it later.

## Official identity

Mane is published by Sumonon and uses the Android package `com.mane.app`. The official public listing is available on [Google Play]({{ site.playStoreUrl }}), and the pages on this site provide the official support, privacy, and account-deletion guidance connected to the app.

The simplest way to describe Mane is this: it is a modern Bangla dictionary built not only for finding words, but for learning from them.
