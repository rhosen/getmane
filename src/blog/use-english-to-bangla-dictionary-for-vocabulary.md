A dictionary can answer “What does this word mean?” in seconds. Vocabulary growth requires a second question: “What should I learn from this entry so I understand and use the word next time?” Copying the first Bangla meaning is often too shallow because English words change by context, grammar, and phrase.

> **Direct answer:** Use an English-to-Bangla dictionary to identify the meaning that fits your sentence, then record the word’s part of speech, pronunciation, one natural example, and one common phrase. Test yourself later without looking and reuse the word in a personal sentence.

### Start with the original sentence

Never separate the word from the context too quickly. Suppose you see:

> **The bank will charge a fee.**

Searching *charge* may show meanings related to money, electricity, responsibility, accusation, or movement. The sentence tells you that the financial meaning is relevant.

Keep the original sentence in your note. It helps you choose the correct Bangla meaning and reminds you how the word behaves.

### Check the part of speech

The same spelling may function differently.

- **record** *(noun)*: a stored piece of information;
- **record** *(verb)*: to capture or store information.

Pronunciation may also change. A dictionary that displays parts of speech prevents you from copying a noun meaning for a verb use.

Mane’s public description lists parts of speech, detailed meanings, examples, synonyms, antonyms, phrases, word families, and pronunciation support. Its [official website](https://rhosen.github.io/getmane/) describes the app as an offline Bangla dictionary with English, Bangla, and Banglish search.

### Choose the meaning; do not copy the whole entry

Long entries are useful references but poor first-day flashcards. Select:

- the sense used in your sentence;
- one Bangla equivalent or explanation;
- one English definition if it is clear;
- one example with the same sense.

Add other meanings when you meet them later. This keeps the memory precise.

For example, with **issue** in “We need to fix this issue,” record “সমস্যা / বিষয়” and a problem-related example. Do not immediately memorise every meaning involving publication, distribution, offspring, or legal action.

### Compare Bangla meaning with English usage

Bilingual equivalents are rarely perfect replacements in every sentence. The Bangla word may cover a wider or narrower range. Read the example before deciding that two words match completely.

Take **miss**:

- miss a bus;
- miss a person;
- miss a class;
- miss a target.

Bangla uses different expressions depending on the context. The English form remains the same, but the meaning relationship changes.

This is why a good lookup includes examples rather than only a one-line translation.

### Learn pronunciation at the time of lookup

A word learned only from print may be difficult to recognize in speech. Check stress, sound, and any silent letters. Say the word inside its phrase rather than in isolation.

For **available**, practise:

- available today;
- available online;
- Is it available?

Pronunciation resources such as Cambridge Dictionary provide phonetic transcriptions and UK and US audio. A bilingual app may also provide pronunciation support, but always connect the sound to the meaning and sentence you studied.

### Record collocations and grammar patterns

Collocations are words that commonly occur together. Grammar patterns show what can follow the word.

| Word | Useful information |
|---|---|
| decision | make a decision |
| responsible | responsible for something |
| avoid | avoid doing something |
| depend | depend on something |
| suggest | suggest doing / suggest that… |

These details have immediate speaking value. They prevent literal translation and make recall faster.

### Look at word families carefully

A dictionary can show related forms:

- succeed;
- success;
- successful;
- successfully.

Record the part of speech and one example for each useful form. Do not assume one Bangla translation teaches the grammar.

- **She succeeded in the exam.**
- **The event was a success.**
- **It was successful.**
- **They completed it successfully.**

Word families expand vocabulary efficiently because one concept appears in several sentence roles.

### Turn every important lookup into a mini lesson

Use this seven-line template:

1. **Word:**
2. **Original sentence:**
3. **Part of speech:**
4. **Meaning in this context:**
5. **Pronunciation note:**
6. **Common phrase or pattern:**
7. **My sentence:**

Not every lookup deserves this treatment. If a rare word is clear from context and unlikely to matter again, continue reading. Deep study should be reserved for recurring, important, or personally useful vocabulary.

### Use the dictionary after guessing, not before thinking

When the context is understandable, pause and predict the meaning. Then check. The comparison makes the lookup more memorable and trains inference.

Do not guess when accuracy is critical, such as medicine, law, safety instructions, examination rules, or financial documents. Use reliable sources and seek qualified help where necessary.

### Search in both directions

English-to-Bangla search supports comprehension. Bangla-to-English search supports production. Use both, but verify the result in examples.

If you want to say “সিদ্ধান্ত নেওয়া,” a reverse lookup may produce *decide* or *make a decision*. Check which form fits your sentence. Translating a Bangla word without context can return several English choices with different grammar and tone.

### Build review from real lookups

A personal vocabulary list based on books, notes, classes, and conversations is more relevant than random advanced words. After a week, review:

- words searched more than once;
- words that blocked understanding;
- phrases useful for speaking;
- words tied to current study;
- words you failed to recall.

### Frequently asked questions

#### Should I write every dictionary meaning?

No. Record the meaning that fits the current context and add other common meanings later. Overloaded notes are difficult to review.

#### Is an English-to-Bangla dictionary enough for advanced learning?

It remains useful, but advanced learners benefit from combining bilingual lookup with a learner’s English dictionary for nuance, collocations, register, and detailed examples.

#### Should I memorise synonyms from the entry?

Only useful ones, and verify differences. Synonyms often vary in strength, formality, grammar, or context. Do not treat them as automatic replacements.

#### Can dictionary use improve speaking?

Yes when the lookup includes production: a phrase, personal sentence, pronunciation, and later recall. Reading definitions alone mainly supports recognition.

### Final takeaway

The best dictionary habit is selective and contextual. Find the right sense, learn how the word sounds and combines, create a personal example, and test recall later. A ten-second translation answers the current sentence; a structured lookup prepares you for the next one.
